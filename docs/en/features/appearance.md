# Appearance

## Animations & Live Settings

- **Open Animations**: Mod screens and popups can fade and scale in when opened; disable or choose between three speeds under **Settings → General → Interface**.
- **Immediate Effect**: Minimap, waypoints, entity radar, layers, and info lines each have independent display settings that take effect immediately.

## Resource Packs & UI Theming

- Toolbar icons and minimap frames can be replaced through standard Minecraft resource packs.
- Compatible assets from **Xaero UI resource packs** continue to work; supported paths and limits are documented in [UI resource packs](https://github.com/Conflux-Union/conflux-map/blob/master/docs/reference-specs/ui-resource-packs.md).

## Update Notices & Survey

- An optional update check announces new versions in chat.
- After several hours of play, a survey invitation may appear in chat, along with links to the QQ group and the Discord server; "Don't remind me again" disables further notices.
